# OAICrew

An open-source collection of AI employees designed to solve real-world problems across different domains.

Each employee is designed to perform a real-world role.

## Domains

### Business
Contains AI employees for business tasks.

### Healthcare
Contains AI employees for healthcare and medical tasks.

### Education
Contains AI employees for learning and teaching.

### Engineering
Contains AI employees for software development.

### Finance
Contains AI employees for financial analysis.

### Science
Contains AI employees for scientific research and analysis.

---

Add employees by creating folders in the appropriate domain.
